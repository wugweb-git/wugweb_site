# Color Roles

## Operational guidance

Black provides authority and framing; white or warm off-white provides clarity; magenta signals human/brand energy; yellow signals focus and opportunity; teal/cyan signals product or digital systems; royal blue signals structure and trust.

Use two to four accent colours per artwork. Keep people and most photography monochrome so colour belongs to the brand system. Avoid unapproved purple, green, orange, neon, rainbow palettes, and gradients.

Limit each artwork to 2–4 accents plus neutral tones.

## Usage matrix

| Mode | Typical palette behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Editorial thought leadership | Off-white/black base plus 2–3 accents. |
| Campaign collage | Black base or off-white base plus 3–4 approved accents. |
| Product proof | Neutral product UI plus one or two structural brand accents. |
| Systems/architecture | Neutral base, one anchor colour, restrained structural blue/cyan. |
| Typographic collateral | High contrast; one headline colour field may dominate. |
