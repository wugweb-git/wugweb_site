# Visual Grammar

## Operational guidance

Wugweb visual grammar follows **meaning → metaphor → anchor → proof**. Start with a business transformation, translate it into a tangible metaphor, establish one dominant anchor, then use people, objects, and product UI as proof.

Circles and arcs imply a field, market, loop, or focus. Rounded cards imply information, capability, or a product boundary. Routes imply movement and decision. A human hand implies judgement or action. None are filler. The design should still communicate its idea when the headline is removed.

Defines the visual language used to communicate systems, transformation and clarity.
