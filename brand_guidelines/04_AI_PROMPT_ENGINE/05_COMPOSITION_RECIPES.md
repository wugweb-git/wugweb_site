# Composition Recipes

## Recipes

- **Transformation:** a route or changing object leads from constrained state to outcome.
- **Navigator:** map, compass, marker, or ribbon directs a person through a journey.
- **Builder:** hands assemble modules, profiles, or product parts.
- **Signal:** an object searches, selects, magnifies, or highlights meaningful information.
- **Editorial statement:** strong type/panel shares space with one hero visual.

Prompt recipes for editorial, collage, diagram, and campaign layouts.
