# Negative Prompts

## Exclude

`robots, android head, AI brain, neural network, circuit board, hologram, cyberpunk, sci-fi city, glowing blue technology, generic SaaS hero, stock corporate team, handshake, dense dashboard, tiny analytics widgets, random icons, node-web background, thin connector abuse, rainbow gradient, clutter, unreadable text, watermark`

Exclusions support a clear concept; they cannot repair an unclear concept.

Avoid AI clichés, clutter, generic tech motifs, and inconsistent branding.
