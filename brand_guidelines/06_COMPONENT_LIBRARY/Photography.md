# Photography

## Operational guidance

Use high-contrast monochrome cut-outs of authentic people, hands, and objects. Choose an active pose that advances the metaphor. Keep edges clean and styling restrained; avoid colourful corporate stock scenes and decorative faces.

## Purpose
Define how Photography should be used within the Wugweb Brand OS.

## Design Principles
- Support the visual narrative.
- Keep an editorial composition.
- Use only when meaningful.
