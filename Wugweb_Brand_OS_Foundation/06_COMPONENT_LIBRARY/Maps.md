# Maps

## Operational guidance

Maps represent discovery, journey, territory, and contextual understanding. Use simple regions, markers, or routes. Avoid literal GPS/dashboard detail; pair with a human act of reading, selecting, or navigating.

## Purpose
Define how Maps should be used within the Wugweb Brand OS.

## Design Principles
- Support the visual narrative.
- Keep an editorial composition.
- Use only when meaningful.
