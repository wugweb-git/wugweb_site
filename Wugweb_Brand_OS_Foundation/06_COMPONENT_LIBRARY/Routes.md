# Routes

## Operational guidance

Routes show decisions or an orchestrated sequence. Use few turns and destinations, with a clear origin and outcome. Do not turn a simple idea into a dense technical process map.

## Purpose
Define how Routes should be used within the Wugweb Brand OS.

## Design Principles
- Support the visual narrative.
- Keep an editorial composition.
- Use only when meaningful.
