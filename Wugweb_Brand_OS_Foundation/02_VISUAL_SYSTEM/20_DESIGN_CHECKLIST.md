# Design Checklist

## Approval gate

- One message and tangible metaphor.
- One dominant anchor and no competing focal point.
- Meaningful human/object/UI relationship.
- Two to four Wugweb accents; controlled photography.
- Rounded language, editorial asymmetry, breathing room.
- No robots, AI brains, dashboard clutter, ambient node webs, or filler.
- Reads at target size and feels art-directed rather than AI-generated.

- Collage-first
- Designer-made
- One focal anchor
- Limited palette
- Concept before decoration
- Avoid AI clichés
