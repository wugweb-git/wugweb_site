# Visual Density

## Operational guidance

Control density through hierarchy, not component count. A premium Wugweb asset has clear breathing room, one focal area, and quiet support.

When work feels weak, remove the least meaningful element first. Do not add grids, icons, labels, lines, or cards to fill space. Use one anchor, one interaction, up to three support layers, and one optional micro-accent as the default.

Prefer clarity over density. Remove non-essential decoration.
