# Photography Direction

## Operational guidance

Use authentic people, hands, and objects in monochrome or tightly controlled treatment. Favour natural gestures and interaction: holding, pointing, searching, building, selecting, navigating, or observing.

Avoid staged smiling teams, handshakes, glossy office stock scenes, colourful clothing that fights the palette, and faces made uncanny by generation tools.

Real, authentic, cut-out, high contrast, minimal backgrounds.
