# Information Architecture as Art

## Operational guidance

Wugweb makes invisible systems visible, but not through generic flowcharts. Turn an architecture, journey, decision, or relationship into a tangible editorial composition: a route, a set of modules being assembled, a selected signal, a map, or an adaptive product moment.

Use literal diagrams only when structure itself is the subject. Otherwise favour a metaphor that makes the system clear at a glance.

Visualize relationships, workflows and systems.
