## NTUC U Care Fund – Digital Transformation for Enhanced Member Support
Subtitle: Empowering low-income families through a holistic digital ecosystem.
Category: Web & App Dev, Experience Design
Tags: Social Impact, Admin Backend, Data Centralization
Partner: iCube
Client: NTUC U Care
Industry: Non-Profit, Social Services
Disciplines: Strategy, UX Design, UI Design, Frontend Development, Backend Development, Systems Integration
Team: Vedanshu Srivastava, Alwyn Lai, Tang Ka Wei, Gadar Soe

### About
NTUC U Care is the National Trades Union Congress initiative in Singapore that supports low-income union members and their families through financial and educational assistance, including the NTUC Care Fund and e-Vouchers. Wugweb, with iCube, rebuilt the platform that delivers that support: a public site members can understand, an applicant portal they can finish, and an admin backend the team can run programmes from.

### The challenge
Help existed, but reaching it was hard for members and running it was hard for staff.

*For members*
- A dated site, hard to navigate and weak on mobile
- Eligibility information that was hard to find
- Applications many people abandoned partway through

*For the NTUC U Care team*
- Programmes, applications and disbursements run on manual, fragmented workflows
- Data spread across separate sources, leaving little room for reporting or planning

### What we built
One platform with two sides.

*For members and applicants*
- A mobile-first site where people can find a programme and check eligibility before applying
- Guided, multi-step applications with clear instructions and visible progress
- Updates and resources delivered through the platform

*For the NTUC U Care team*
- One admin backend to create programmes, process applications and oversee disbursements
- A single source of truth for programme and beneficiary data

### How we approached it
- Replace fragmented manual steps with one system, rather than polishing each step on its own
- Build each journey around the task in front of that user: member, applicant or administrator
- Treat accessibility and mobile as requirements, in line with NTUC U Care's mission

### The outcome
- Members find and apply for assistance on any device with far less friction
- The team runs programmes from one system, with the data to report on them

### FAQ
What did Wugweb build for NTUC U Care?
A mobile-first public platform, a guided applicant portal and an admin backend for programmes, applications and aid disbursement, on one central data model.
Who delivered the NTUC U Care platform?
Wugweb (strategy, UX/UI, frontend, backend and systems integration) in partnership with iCube.
Who uses the platform?
Low-income union members and their families applying for assistance, and the NTUC U Care team running the programmes.
