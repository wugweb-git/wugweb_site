## Response

Yes, an email you wrote is the right kind of sample, and you supplying it is the consent the ledger needs. One problem first: the message reached me with "[text]" where the email should be, so I have nothing to read yet. Please paste the email, or tell me where the original lives.

Nothing has been recorded so far. When the text arrives, this is what happens.

The email's text is not copied into the skill. The row points to where the original lives. A short excerpt goes in only with your yes.

**2. Cleaning before reading**

- Only your own lines are kept. A quoted reply, a greeting from someone else or a signature block from another person is left out before I read, and the row notes what was removed.
- Typos are cleaned first. They are never copied as voice.
- Boilerplate (a footer, a standard sign-off block) is left out.

**3. What I read for**

Vocabulary, phrasing, sentence length and cadence, formality, punctuation, openings and endings.

**4. What one email can and cannot do**

- It cannot make a rule. An observed rule needs at least three unedited samples in the mode, with the feature holding steady as samples are added. What I see in one email stays a note.
- Confidence for `email` stays low. I will not write "in your voice" from it.
- Anything I do notice comes back to you as a question. Only your yes confirms a rule.

**Four things I need from you**

1. The email text, or its location.
2. The date you wrote it.
3. Did anyone else edit it, or was any of it drafted with AI?
4. Use it for rules, or hold it back as unseen text for the blind test later? (Suggested share held back: one in five; with one sample, I would use it.)

More emails help most: three or more unedited ones would let me start asking you about patterns.
