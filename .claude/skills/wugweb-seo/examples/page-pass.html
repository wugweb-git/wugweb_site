A booking flow families finish in one sitting

Wugweb rebuilt the booking flow for a care provider so that families can choose a programme, a date and a carer in one sitting. The old flow asked for the same details on three separate screens and lost most people at the second step. The new flow keeps every choice on one page and shows the price before the family commits.
