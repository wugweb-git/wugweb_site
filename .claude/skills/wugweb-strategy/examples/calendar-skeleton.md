# Example: calendar skeleton

A reusable skeleton for one month. No topics or dates are filled: every row is a slot waiting for the owner's input. Cadence is "owner to set" (decisions 8 and 18).

**Mode:** Calendar. **Owner to set:** pieces per account per month, promotional share, reactive share.

Checks before proposing: no two asks in a row on one account; promotion a minority; each row has one pillar, one intent, one audience, one account, even a slot row, where the pillar and intent are proposals; dependencies named; every date marked proposed.
