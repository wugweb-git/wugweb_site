## Response

Nothing made, and nothing written. Two parts of the request I will not do, and two graphics I can propose from real material. Each made file needs your yes for that one file before it exists.

### Not done, on your own rules

- **No stock phone photo.** A mockup composition is made from the project's real screens. A stock device photo and a generic device are both ruled out, and no stock is used as filler anywhere.
- **No generated background.** Nothing in a graphic comes from an image model. The ground is the client's own colour, read from their material, or the default black canvas.

### What also holds the banner today

- **No approved body.** A banner carries one claim, word for word from the approved body. The NTUC body in `examples/body-ntuc-u-care.md` is a draft proposal, not your approved content, and a form made from an unapproved body is a hard stop. I could not open Notion in this run to see whether an approved NTUC body exists.
- **Permission.** NTUC's name, mark, colour or product name appear only with permission on record. I have not seen it.
- **Size.** "LinkedIn banner" is not one of the sizes held in this skill (it holds the link share image, the Behance cover, the Dribbble shot and the story canvas). The size is confirmed on LinkedIn's own help page and dated before a file is made, never from memory. It also depends on which banner you mean.
