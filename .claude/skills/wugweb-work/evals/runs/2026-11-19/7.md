### The brief fields I need

The brief comes from `wugweb-strategy`. I check it; I do not write it. All six of these must be there:

1. The reader.
2. What they know now.
3. What they should know and do after reading.
4. The goal of the post.
5. The format.
6. Which case studies back it.

Also used when the brief has them, though their absence does not stop the draft: the pages the post links to, dates, the `wugweb-seo` keyword note, and the approved FAQ questions or the line "no FAQ block".

### What this post also needs before a draft

1. Proof. A claim about a client project must match that project's approved case study body, and the post links to it. I could not read the Kia case study in this run, so I cannot say what it already publishes. A published v1 body counts for the facts it publishes; anything beyond that needs an approved v2 body or your answer.
2. Permission. A post that explains client work uses that client's screens only with permission on record, and a client is not named without it. Is there permission on record to name Kia and show the dashboard screens?
3. Sensitivity. "How we built it" touches client process detail, which I ask you about before it enters a draft.
4. "We" or "I" for this post?

### What happens once the brief arrives

1. Check the brief against the six fields. A missing one goes back to `wugweb-strategy`.
2. Audit what exists and give one verdict.
3. Measure and look at the cover and inline images already in the media store.
4. Outline, then draft to the blog post body standard: the claim and who it is for up front, one teachable thing, claim headings with the point first and then the evidence, limits or trade-offs, a close with one next step.
5. Fact lock, evidence ledger, voice check, title and description limits.
6. Propose it to you as current to proposed. The source is written only after you approve that exact text, with a backup first.

The blog post body standard is a first draft awaiting your review, not a final standard. Any post written from it will be marked "draft standard".
