## Response

No chart: the NTUC body holds no result to plot. I have made no file and invented no figure. Nothing was written anywhere.

### Why

A chart is made only from numbers the body already holds in full. I checked the NTUC body in the skill's example:

- Results lines: 0. There is no `Results` key and no Results chapter.
- The fact pack made from it records "Results: 0 (the body has no result with a source and date)".
- The proposal itself left out the old copy's result claims because nothing backed them, and its first owner question asks for "any number with its source and date".
- The figures the body does carry are not results: a year, counts of sections, templates and steps on the screens, and a colour contrast ratio. None measures an outcome.

Two more stops apply:

- That body is a proposal you have not approved. A form is made only from an approved body.
- A chart for a deck is a made file. It needs your yes for that one file, after it is proposed.

### What a number needs before it can be charted

All six parts, for each number:

1. Value.
2. What it measures.
3. Period.
4. Baseline or benchmark.
5. Source.
6. Date (as of).

A number missing any part is not charted. A rate also needs its denominator and its definition. A benchmark needs its source, year and population and is never shown as the client's result. One or two numbers are a stat, not a chart.

### What I need from you

1. The results, each with the six parts above. The proposal suggested these as useful, if they exist with a source: programmes live on the platform, applications handled, processing time before and after, launch date. "Unvalidated" or "none" is a complete answer.
2. Approval of the NTUC body, with those numbers added as Results lines.
3. Whether the deck is read alone or presented.

### What happens then

I list the qualifying numbers, propose the chart (type, axes, units and the source line printed under it), and ask for your yes for that one file before it is made. Every plotted value will equal the value in the body.

Until then the deck can run without a results slide: a deck carries results only when they exist.
