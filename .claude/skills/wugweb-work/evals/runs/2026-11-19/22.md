## Response

No colour or font picked, and nothing written. A client's brand values are never chosen by eye or from memory: they come from you with the content, or they are read from what exists and proposed to you. I could do neither in this run, so here is what happens next and what I need.

### Where the values come from, in order

1. **Your content.** The body's `Brand colours`, `Brand fonts` and `Accent` lines. If the Inkompass body holds them, they are used as given and not second-guessed. I could not open the Notion body in this run, so I do not know whether it does.
2. **If the body holds none: a brand read, proposed to you.** Read from what already exists, each value recorded with where it was read:
   - Inkompass's live stylesheet: their declared colour tokens, the colour on their primary action, the colours that cover the most area, and their font families with roles and weights.
   - The pixels of the project's own screens in the case study's folder: the colours that recur, leaving out near-white, near-black and grey.
   - Any brand file in the folder: a palette sheet, a type specimen, a guideline page.
3. **Your yes.** Nothing I read goes on a page until you have put it in the content. Where a read value differs from one you supplied, yours stands and I report the difference.

The brand read was not run: I had no access to their site, the media folder or the body here, and each read needs your go.

### What the proposal will contain when it is run

| Item | Rule it must pass |
|---|---|
| Accent | One colour, taken from their primary action or declared brand token. At 3:1 or more against the page surface for rules, numerals, labels and markers; used as text only at 4.5:1 or more. The ratio is written unrounded |
| Up to two more colours | For a ground behind screens, one brand band, or a chapter tint. The text colour on each is computed, not assumed |
| A colour that fails contrast | Shifted in lightness for use here, with the shift noted. Their mark is never recoloured |
| Fonts | Named in the copy and the Styles caption, and shown through their own screens and type specimen. A client font file is never loaded on this site without a licence on record |
| If nothing can be read | No colour is invented. The house accent is used and the gap stays an owner question |

"Looks like their brand" has a limit: the Wugweb system stays the frame (type, spacing, header, footer), and yellow stays Wugweb's. Inside the body, their colour, icons and brand assets are used on purpose.

### What I need from you

1. Does the Inkompass body already carry `Brand colours`, `Brand fonts` or `Accent`? If so, paste them or let me read the body.
2. If not: your go for a brand read, and the address of the Inkompass site or product to read (the `Brand site` line). A page behind a login is not read.
3. Is there a licence on record for their typefaces? Without one they are named and shown in their screens, not loaded.
4. Is there permission on record to show Inkompass's mark, colour and product name on the page?
