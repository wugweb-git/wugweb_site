## Response

This is the block as the skill specifies it, applied to the NTUC proposal in `examples/body-ntuc-u-care.md`. I have not rendered the page or opened it in this run, so nothing below is "seen on a render". Nothing was written.

### Where it sits

One block titled "All screens", near the end of the page: after the last chapter and before credits. NTUC has no results, so nothing sits between the chapters and it. It holds every approved screen, so nothing is lost when a chapter shows only its proof.

### The chips

A row of five set chips, each with its count. One chip is selected at a time, and the grid below shows that set.

| Chip | Count | What the set name is |
|---|---|---|
| Member | 8 | An audience |
| States | 4 | A state group |
| Dashboard | 9 | A kind of screen |
| Back office | 7 | An audience (staff); this is my reading, the skill does not name it |
| Cases | 3 | A flow |

- Total 31. The 12 proof screens are here as well as in their chapters. The 2 hidden screens (`Group 17563`, `Group 17616`) are not in the block.
- Order: audience first, then flow.
- Behind each chip the record keeps audience, flow, and kind and state apart. The chip label is never stored as the only category.
- Chips use the full radius. The chip row may scroll sideways on a phone; the page never does.
- Chips sit inside the block they filter, not in a section of their own.

### The thumbnails

- Each thumbnail keeps its own ratio. Nothing is cropped or reshaped.
- On a phone: 3 columns for phone screens, 1 to 2 columns for desktop screens. Denser on desktop.
- Every thumbnail carries its width and height and loads lazily. In the proposal the 19 screens that are only in this block add 7.5 MB, loaded only when near.
- Each thumbnail is a target of 44 px or more and opens the viewer at that screen.

### What the viewer shows

One screen at a time, in a native dialog:

- The screen at its natural size, at least twice the thumbnail. Pinch zoom is allowed.
- The caption, as written in the body. For the third Member screen: "LIFT-UP Pathfinder page on the same programme template".
- The position and the set: "3 of 8, Member".
- A visible close button, and previous and next buttons.
- No sound. If a video is ever in the viewer it is muted, with a play and pause button only.

### How you move through it

- Previous and next are real, named buttons of 44 px or more. Swipe also works, but it is an extra, never the only way.
- Escape closes it. So does the close button, and so does the back action.
- While it is open, focus is trapped inside it. On close, focus returns to the thumbnail that opened it.
- Every screen has its own deep link, which opens the viewer at that screen.
- Moving runs within the selected set: 1 of 8 to 8 of 8 for Member, and so on.

### With JavaScript off

- All five sets render stacked, each under its own heading, in this order: Member, States, Dashboard, Back office, Cases.
- Every thumbnail and every caption is in the server HTML. All 31 screens are reachable.
- There are no working chips and no viewer. They are an improvement on top, never the only way to reach a screen.

### What is not settled or not built

- Not buildable yet: the manifest record in the CMS has no field for a set name. The resolver accepts one, the schema does not hold it yet.
- Flagged screens in the block: 12 of the 31 carry a flag (placeholder text or sample personal data) and stay because each is the only proof of something. The names on `Group 17566` and the dashboard set are for you to clear before the page ships.
