### 4. Proposed page (draft standard)

Structure, held throughout: what it does and for whom, the problem it removes, how it works, what you receive, limits, one action. Voice: "we", as the current record uses; your pick is asked below.

```
Label: Brand

# What a rebrand involves, from audit to guidelines

We take a B2B start-up through a rebrand as one piece of work: positioning, identity and the guidelines your team will use.

[Book a call]

## You know the brand has to change. The work itself is less clear.

A rebrand is more than a new logo. It decides what the company stands for, how it sounds and how it looks. This page sets out what we do, in what order, and what you receive at each stage.

## The work runs in five stages

1. **Discovery**: We audit the brand as it stands, study your audience and review your competitors. You get a clear view of the brand today and where it can go.
2. **Strategy**: We set the positioning, the messaging and the brand architecture. Naming is part of this stage when the work needs it.
3. **Identity**: We design the logo system and the visual language, and write the voice and tone.
4. **Activation**: We prepare the marketing assets, the digital experiences and the launch plan.
5. **Evolution**: We review the brand over time and refine the identity as the business changes.

## What you receive

**Positioning and messaging**
What the company stands for and how it says so.

**A name, where one is needed**
For the company, a product or a service.

**A visual identity system**
Logo, typography, colour and imagery, designed to work together.

**Voice and tone**
How the brand writes and speaks.

**Brand guidelines**
Practical guidelines, templates and handover sessions, so design, marketing and product can apply the brand.

## The identity is designed to hold up in the product

We design brand systems for product interfaces, marketing sites and campaigns, not only for a brand book.

## Where this fits, and where it does not

We do not design a logo by itself. A logo comes as part of a full identity system.

Not every company needs a full rebrand. We also refresh an existing identity: a tighter system, updated assets and a clearer voice, keeping what already works.

## Book a call

Tell us where the brand is today. We will say which stages you need.

[Book a call]
```
