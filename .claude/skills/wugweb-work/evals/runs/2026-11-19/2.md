### How to fill it

1. Replace each marker with your fact, or delete the line. "Unvalidated" is a complete answer to any marker.
2. A metric in the full form has six parts: value, what it measures, period, baseline or benchmark, source and as-of date. In the Results line they sit in four slots: `value | what, with period and baseline | source | as of`. A number without a source is not a result. It stays out, or it is shown as "unvalidated".
3. A rate needs its denominator and its definition. A benchmark needs its source, year and population, and is never shown as the client's result.
4. Two to four verified results sit under the hero as stats. The rest go to the Results chapter.
5. One chapter per workstream that has its own proof. A workstream with no proof loses its chapter.
6. Headings are claims of 70 characters or fewer. The first sentence answers the heading.
7. The quote is used only with a named person, the exact wording and the person's consent. Otherwise delete both lines.
8. Charts are drawn on the page as HTML or SVG from the Results lines. No chart image is made.
9. No FAQ block: a case study never carries one.
