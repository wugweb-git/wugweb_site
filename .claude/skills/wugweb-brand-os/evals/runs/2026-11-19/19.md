## Response

Mode: Answer. File: `references/messaging.md`.

The framework is how a message is built. Part of it is fixed, and part of it is blank.

**Fixed**

| Rule | Label |
| --- | --- |
| Every asset has one claim, one audience tension, one proof and one outcome | Confirmed, fixed. Source: `brand_guidelines/03_MARKETING_SYSTEM/02_MESSAGING_FRAMEWORK.md` |
| The order is Problem to Insight to System to Outcome | Confirmed, fixed. Same source |
| The client is the hero, Wugweb is the guide | Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-17 (question 13) |

The visual expresses the claim through a tangible metaphor and the copy names it. A thumbnail is never asked to carry a whole positioning framework.

**Blank**

**Core message and supports: not decided, the owner decides.** The core message depends on the positioning line, which is blank (question 1). The shape waiting to be filled: one core message with three or four supports, each support with one proof from `references/brand-facts.md` or a named case study. A support without proof is cut. Any message ladder built today is a proposal, because positioning comes first on the ladder: positioning, one main message, three or four supports, a proof for each.

**How a message is built and tested** (method, restated from helper skills; it tests a message and never writes one)

- Four questions, in order: why should I care, what is it, why Wugweb, what do I do next.
- Differences are stated against every alternative, including doing nothing.
- Six tests: one of each (claim, tension, proof, outcome); the message order; proof matched to the likely objection; three-second clarity; ownable ("we can own this because", finished with a named fact); every number, name and quote through the fact check.
- Proof strength for a single piece, weakest to strongest: a bare rating, a short quote, a named quote with role and organisation, a number with its source and date, a full case study.
- Proof rules: nothing made up, permission recorded, meaning kept when a quote is trimmed, every fact about Wugweb taken from `references/brand-facts.md`.
- A message passes when it is clear, different, credible, relevant and consistent.

**Not here**

- Wording, tone and style: `wugweb-voice`.
- What to publish and when: `wugweb-strategy`.
- The audience whose tension a message names: not decided, the owner decides (question 3).
