# Eval 3: answer: fixed rule with its file

Prompt: How much yellow can I use on a service page banner?

Files read: `SKILL.md`, `references/standing-rules.md`, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md`, `references/visual-os/art-direction.md`, `references/source-map.md`

## Response

Mode: Answer.

Very little, and it depends which banner you mean. The rule is **confirmed** and held in `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md`:

- Yellow is the one brand colour and an accent at 10% or less.
- Yellow is a reservation, not an emphasis level. It is the resting fill in only two places: the one conversion action per page, and the footer banner. The final call to action stays yellow.
- Other buttons may use yellow only on hover or tap.
- On a yellow surface, the button inverts to dark.

So:

| If the banner is | Yellow |
| --- | --- |
| The footer banner | Yes. It is one of the two places where yellow is the resting fill. Its button inverts to dark |
| Any other banner on the service page (hero, mid-page, pillar section) | Not as a fill. Yellow appears only on the page's one conversion action, and the page as a whole stays at 10% or less |

For the rest of a service page banner: structure stays in brand ink, an accent is spent on one or two things, and the pillar colour marks its pillar without painting structure. The pillar glow is the one allowed gradient exception on service pages (question 16, confirmed 2026-11-17). Text is never pure white.

The exact yellow, the percentages as measured and the banner component belong to `wugweb-kits`. No value is written here.

If your banner is neither the footer banner nor the conversion action and you want it yellow, that is a change to a confirmed rule and goes through Change mode for your approval.
