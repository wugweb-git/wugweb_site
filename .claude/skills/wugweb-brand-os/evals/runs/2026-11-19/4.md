# Eval 4: visual check: AI brain

Prompt: Check this blog hero brief: a glowing blue AI brain made of circuit lines in the centre, with the headline 'Smarter products with AI' set inside the image.

Files read: `SKILL.md`, `references/standing-rules.md`, `references/review-gates.md`, `references/visual-os/judging-a-visual.md`, `references/visual-os/art-direction.md`, `references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md`, `references/visual-os/metaphors.md`, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md`, `references/messaging.md`

## Response

Mode: Visual check, on a written brief only. No artwork was supplied, so no artwork was seen. Everything below judges the brief.

**Result: fail.**

**Overall impression.** The brief asks for the exact image the brand bans: an AI symbol standing in for an AI idea. It also puts the headline inside a format that carries none. Nothing in it is specific to a product, a client or an outcome, so it would come out the same for any AI article.

**What the brief describes**

| Part | As written |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | An AI brain made of circuit lines |
| Composition | Centred, with the headline inside the image |
| Lighting | Glowing |
| Colour | Blue |
| Style | Not stated |
| Mood | Not stated |

**Mode.** A blog hero defaults to editorial thought leadership (`references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md`). The brief does not meet it: that mode is one visual sentence with generous space and little or no text.

**Two-second test.** The eye would go to the glowing brain. That says "AI" and nothing about what the product does better. With the headline removed, no claim is left.

**Findings**

| No. | Where | What | Rule and file | Severity | Fix, current to proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Subject | An AI brain | Banned: robots, AI brains, neural webs, circuitry and holograms are never used, least of all as a shortcut for AI. `SKILL.md` fixed rules; banned list in `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Must fix | AI brain to the Intelligence metaphor in `references/visual-os/metaphors.md`: selected signals, knowledge cards, adaptive routes, human judgement. Show the outcome (navigation, recommendation, discovery), not an AI symbol |
| 2 | Subject | Made of circuit lines | Banned: circuit boards and circuitry. Same files | Must fix | Circuit lines to nothing. Remove, do not swap for another technology texture |
| 3 | Lighting and colour | Glowing blue | Banned: "glowing blue technology". The only allowed glow is the pillar glow (question 16); other gradient-heavy looks stay banned. `references/visual-os/art-direction.md`, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md` | Must fix | Glowing blue to the black canvas with a neutral base and two or three accents, the palette for this mode |
| 4 | Headline | Set inside the image | Blog hero: text in the artwork is none; the headline sits outside the image. `references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md` | Must fix | Headline inside the image to headline as live text outside it |
| 5 | Concept | No stated claim, tension, proof or outcome | Every asset has one of each, and the idea survives with the headline removed. `references/messaging.md`, `references/review-gates.md` | Must fix | State the four before any visual choice. Which smarter product, and what changed for its user? |
| 6 | Composition | Centred | Asymmetrical balance around one anchor; avoid centred symmetry. `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Should fix | Centred subject to an off-centre anchor with true negative space |
| 7 | Proof | None | A real photo or screenshot comes before generated imagery; a blog hero allows none or one proof module. `references/review-gates.md` | Should fix | If a real product is the subject, one credible module of it. Otherwise none |

**Quality gates** (`references/review-gates.md`, taken in order, stop at the first that fails): the concept gate fails, because no idea survives without the headline. The compliance gate would also fail on banned motifs.

**What works.** One subject, and no clutter of extra parts. Keep that restraint.

**Top three fixes, in order**

1. Remove the brain and the circuitry, and restate the claim as an outcome.
2. Choose one tangible object or act from the Intelligence metaphor as the anchor, off centre, on the black canvas.
3. Take the headline out of the image.

**Not judged**

- The artwork itself: craft, legibility at final size, hierarchy in black and white, rights labels. No score out of 20 is given, because a score needs the render.
- The headline wording "Smarter products with AI": that is for `wugweb-voice`.
- Lighting as a rule: not decided, the owner decides. No approved source covers lighting; the finding above rests on the banned list, not on a lighting rule.

These are findings for whoever holds the brief in `wugweb-work`. I have not rewritten the brief and no image is made here.
