## Response

Mode: Visual check, on a written brief only. No artwork was supplied, so no artwork was seen. The result below is for the brief; the render still needs its own check.

**Result: pass, as a brief.** No must-fix finding.

**Overall impression.** The brief is built the way the brand asks: one anchor, one real proof, one human act, one accent, and room around them. The subject is the project's own screen, not a generic device, so it meets the cover rule. What is left is to state a few things the brief leaves unsaid.

**Mode.** Product proof: one credible route card module as evidence of a real product experience. That is one of the two default modes for a case-study cover (`references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md`). No second mode is blended in.

**Two-second test, as briefed.** The eye should go to the circle and the hand with the route card. That is the right first thing, and "a person finds their route" reads without the title. To be confirmed on the render.

**What works**

- One anchor, and geometry that is rounded.
- A project-specific proof: the real route card, which is also the natural object for a Navigation metaphor (`references/visual-os/metaphors.md`).
- The hand is doing something, holding, which is the reason a hand is allowed.
- One accent, used once, as a marker.
- True negative space, and the title kept outside the image.

**Top three fixes, in order**

1. Write the one claim the route card proves.
2. Name the part of the screen the mask must not cover, and confirm the file is shown uncropped at its own aspect ratio.
3. Record rights for the hand photograph and approval for the client's screen.

**Not decided**

- Lighting: not decided, the owner decides. No approved source covers it; on the render it can only be checked for consistency.

**Not judged**

- The render: actual hierarchy, hierarchy in black and white, legibility at final size, craft, and whether it looks art-directed and not machine-made. No score is given without it.
- Whether the hand photograph exists already. A real photo comes first; a new image file is made only when nothing existing fits, one file at a time, on your yes.
- The title's wording: `wugweb-voice`. Any claim about the client: the case study in `wugweb-work`.

Findings go back to `wugweb-work`. The brief has not been rewritten.
