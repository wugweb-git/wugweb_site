# Eval 25: visual: yellow on every card

Prompt: A button panel in the brand yellow on every card

Files read: SKILL.md, references/standing-rules.md, references/review-gates.md, references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md, references/visual-os/judging-a-visual.md

## Response

Mode: Visual check, on a written brief only. No render was supplied, so the artwork was not seen.

**Result: fail as briefed.** One must fix.

Overall impression: the brief spends the brand's one reserved colour on every card. Yellow then stops marking the single action that matters on the page.

| Where | What | Rule | Severity | Fix: current to proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Every card | A button panel with a resting yellow fill | Yellow is a reservation, not an emphasis level. It is the resting fill only on the one conversion action per page and on the footer banner; other buttons may use it only on hover or tap (confirmed, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md`) | Must fix | Yellow panel at rest on every card, to panels in brand ink at rest with yellow on hover or tap only |
| The page as a whole | Yellow repeated across all cards | Yellow is an accent at 10% or less, and an accent is spent on one or two things (confirmed, same file; gate 7 in `references/review-gates.md`) | Must fix, same cause | Many yellow areas, to one: the page's single conversion action, plus the footer banner |

What works: wanting a clear action on each card is sound. The action can stay; only its resting colour changes.

Top fixes, in order:

1. Take the resting yellow off the card buttons.
2. Name the one conversion action on the page and keep yellow there. The final call to action stays yellow.
3. If a card must sit on a yellow surface, its button inverts to dark (same file).

Not judged, because only a brief was supplied: the real share of yellow on the page, hover and tap behaviour, contrast, and how it reads at final size. If the cards belong to a client's case study, that client's accent is a matter for the case study, not this check.

Boundary: the token, the button component and the measured check belong to `wugweb-kits`. This is a finding for whoever is building it; nothing has been changed.
