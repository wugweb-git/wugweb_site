**3. What should the campaign visuals look like?**

This part is decided. First, one ruling: Wugweb has no campaign system (owner, 2026-11-17), so none is defined here and none is reported as missing. The direction for campaign pieces is:

- **Mode:** campaign collage, for social campaigns, collateral, posters and announcements. Bolder type panels, wider approved colour, device frames, photography and controlled overlap, still with one hierarchy and one metaphor. Pick the one mode per piece and do not blend modes. A type-led piece uses typographic presentation.
- **Every piece:** one claim, one tension, one proof, one outcome; one dominant anchor; rounded geometry; asymmetry and true negative space; monochrome people or hands only when they are doing something.
- **Canvas and colour:** black canvas. Three or four approved accents in campaign collage. Yellow is the one brand colour, at 10% or less. Text is never pure white.
- **Holding a campaign together:** the same palette, cut-out treatment, rounded geometry and type across pieces; the metaphor and hero object change from post to post. A small named family of recurring formats (quote card, carousel frame, announcement, cover). A family look is not a clone.
- **Recognition:** readable in two seconds at feed size; one memorable object; a stranger should not be able to name the template.
- **Never:** robots, AI brains, neural webs, circuitry, holograms, node webs as texture, icon clouds, stock teams and handshakes, clutter, anything copied from another studio.
