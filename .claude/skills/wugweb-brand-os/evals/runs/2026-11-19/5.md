# Eval 5: visual check: two modes mixed

Prompt: Check this LinkedIn card brief: a dense connector grid with nodes across the whole canvas, three phone mockups, a large poster headline and a collage of four portraits. Topic: our brand workshop offer.

Files read: `SKILL.md`, `references/standing-rules.md`, `references/review-gates.md`, `references/visual-os/judging-a-visual.md`, `references/visual-os/art-direction.md`, `references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md`, `references/visual-os/objects-and-components.md`, `references/visual-os/metaphors.md`, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md`, `references/messaging.md`, `references/audience.md`

## Response

Mode: Visual check, on a written brief only. No artwork was supplied, so no artwork was seen.

**Result: fail.**

**Overall impression.** The brief blends four visual modes on one card and has no anchor. Each part is the signature of a different mode, so four things compete for first place and none of them says what the workshop offer is. It also carries a banned motif: a node grid used as texture.

**What the brief describes**

| Part | As written |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | Four at once: a connector grid, three phones, a headline, four portraits |
| Composition | Grid across the whole canvas; no stated anchor or negative space |
| Lighting | Not stated |
| Colour | Not stated |
| Style | Collage, poster type |
| Mood | Not stated |

**Mode.** No single mode can be named, and that is the fail (`references/review-gates.md`, point 1). The parts map as follows (`references/visual-os/art-direction.md`):

| Element | Belongs to |
| --- | --- |
| Dense connector grid with nodes | Systems and architecture |
| Three phone mockups | Product proof |
| Large poster headline | Typographic presentation |
| Collage of four portraits | Campaign collage |

A LinkedIn static post defaults to campaign collage or typographic presentation, with a short statement as graphic form and one credible module (`references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md`).

**Two-second test.** Cannot be passed as briefed. There is no one thing for the eye to hit, and with the headline removed no claim about a brand workshop remains.

**Findings**

| No. | Where | What | Rule and file | Severity | Fix, current to proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whole card | Four modes together | Pick one of the five modes and do not blend them. `SKILL.md` fixed rules; `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Must fix | Four modes to one. For this format, campaign collage or typographic presentation; drop the other modes' elements |
| 2 | Background | Dense connector grid with nodes across the whole canvas | Banned: ambient node webs and connector lines as texture. Grids, nodes and connectors are allowed only when the subject is a system, architecture, blueprint or network. A brand workshop offer is not. `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Must fix | Grid across the canvas to none. Leave the space empty; never fill negative space with connectors |
| 3 | Whole card | No anchor | One dominant anchor per composition, roughly 30 to 60% of the canvas, no competing focal point. `SKILL.md`; `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Must fix | Four equal elements to one anchor with quieter supports |
| 4 | Devices | Three phone mockups | A device frame is allowed when it shows an actual interaction, normally one device; a device is never decoration. A workshop is not a product screen. `references/visual-os/objects-and-components.md` | Must fix | Three phones to none, unless one real screen is the proof of the claim |
| 5 | People | A collage of four portraits | A person appears only when showing agency, judgement or interaction; avoid decorative faces and stock teams. Photography is monochrome cut-outs in an active pose. `references/visual-os/art-direction.md`, `references/visual-os/colour-type-photo-motion.md` | Should fix | Four portraits to, at most, one monochrome person or hand doing the thing the workshop does |
| 6 | Concept | No claim, tension, proof or outcome stated | One of each per asset. `references/messaging.md` | Must fix | State the four before choosing visuals |
| 7 | Density | Four element groups, about nine objects | Default is one anchor, one interaction, up to three support layers. Remove, do not add. `references/visual-os/art-direction.md` | Should fix | Remove the least meaningful element first, then the next |
| 8 | Feed size | Small nodes, three small screens, four faces | LinkedIn post: one memorable silhouette; remove detail that will not survive feed size. `references/visual-os/formats-and-campaigns.md` | Should fix | Follows from fixes 1 to 4 |
| 9 | Rights | Four identifiable people | No identifiable person without approval; every photograph carries a rights label. `references/review-gates.md` | Should fix | Record the label and the approval for any portrait kept |

**Quality gates** (`references/review-gates.md`): the concept gate fails first, so the later gates are not run. A weak concept is not fixed by craft.

**What works.** The poster headline suits the format: a short statement as graphic form is allowed on a LinkedIn static post. A hands-on subject such as a workshop also lends itself to a tangible object.

**Top three fixes, in order**

1. State the one claim for the workshop offer and pick one mode.
2. Remove the node grid and the phones.
3. Build around one anchor. If the mode is typographic presentation, the headline is the anchor; if campaign collage, one object or one active person is. The Construction and Creativity rows in `references/visual-os/metaphors.md` are places to look. Which to use is the brief writer's choice.

**Not decided**

- Who the card is for. The audience is not decided: the owner supplies it (question 3, `references/audience.md`). So the "audience tension" cannot be checked, and that point is neither a pass nor a fail.

**Not judged**

- The artwork: craft, colour, yellow share, text colour, hierarchy in black and white, legibility at final size. No score is given without the render.
- The headline's wording: `wugweb-voice`.
- Whether and when to post the offer: `wugweb-strategy`.

Findings go back to the brief's author in `wugweb-work`. Nothing has been rewritten or made.
